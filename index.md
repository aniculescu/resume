## Human Summary

I'm an engineering leader who likes building organizations around hard technical problems.
Over the last 15+ years, I've led engineering teams and organizations across security, identity, infrastructure, SaaS, aviation, and games. My work has ranged from rebuilding and scaling established organizations to starting new teams around problems where neither the technology nor the organizational model was obvious yet.

At 1Password, my scope has grown from leading backend and R&D teams to building the Confidential Computing platform and its engineering organization, and then into broader leadership across identity and security platforms. I lead a 30+ IC engineering organization through three Engineering Managers, spanning Provisioning Product + Platform, Identity Platform, Machine Identity / AI Identity, and Confidential Computing. Together, these platforms support 1Password's mission to secure access for every human and AI agent.

I've spent a lot of my career working with experienced engineers and engineering leaders: hiring and developing managers, supporting Staff+ ICs, creating durable team structures, clarifying ownership, and helping teams turn ambiguous technical work into products that can operate reliably for the long term.
Before 1Password, I led engineering within Panasonic Avionics' Connectivity organization, supporting 30+ engineers through multiple managers across North America and Europe while delivering software for 300+ airline customers.

Earlier at NCSOFT, I grew from an individual contributor into engineering and technical leadership for the North American and European publishing organization.

The work I enjoy most sits between people, technology, and strategy: build the right organization, give strong leaders room to lead, get clear on the problem, and ship something that lasts.

## Detailed Work Experience

### Engineering Leader @ 1Password {#onepassword}

⏳ since 2021
{: .dates}

1Password is an identity security company securing access for humans, AI agents, and machines. Its Unified Access platform helps organizations discover, secure, and audit access across identities, credentials, secrets, applications, and infrastructure.
{: .company}

Across these assignments, my role has increasingly centered on organizational design, manager and Staff+ development, technical and product strategy, and establishing durable ownership for complex platform work.

- Lead a 30+ engineer identity and security platform organization through three direct Engineering Managers, with two of those leaders also managing additional Engineering Managers. Scope spans Provisioning Product + Platform, Identity Platform, [Machine Identity / AI Identity](https://1password.com/solutions/ai-agent-identity), and Confidential Computing.
- Built 1Password's [Confidential Computing](https://1password.com/blog/confidential-computing) platform and engineering team from the ground up, starting with a small R&D group of Staff+ engineers. Led the IC team through development completion, hired its Engineering Manager, and supported the organization through GA, its first production use cases, and subsequent key-persistence capabilities; represented the work externally at [AWS re:Invent](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QBQawVrxqXg).
- Expanded leadership across Authentication, Provisioning, Identity, and Authorization, repeatedly establishing clearer ownership, durable leadership structures, and long-term operating models as the mandate grew.
- Reset Authentication around resiliency and operational stability through staffing, organizational, and technical-priority changes, materially improving reliability and significantly reducing production incidents; hired the team's Engineering Manager to establish sustainable leadership.
- Led engineering for [Automated Provisioning](https://support.1password.com/provisioning/) across technical direction, roadmap, execution, GA readiness, and operational reliability; reorganized team ownership and operating practices to improve delivery predictability. The product reached GA in March 2026.
- Built a new Identity organization in 2025, initially leading seven engineers directly before hiring its Engineering Manager; later expanded the mandate to include Authorization and led development of the [Agentic Identity and Authorization strategy and vision](https://1password.com/blog/introducing-1password-unified-access).
- Contributed at company level through Staff+ leadership development, hiring and interview-system improvements, engineering career frameworks, and technical culture.

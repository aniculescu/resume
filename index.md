## Detailed Work Experience

### Engineering Manager, Unified Identity @ 1Password {#onepassword}

⏳ June 2021–Present
{: .dates}

1Password is an identity security company securing access for humans, AI agents, and machines. Its Unified Access platform helps organizations discover, secure, and audit access across identities, credentials, secrets, applications, and infrastructure.
{: .company}

Across these assignments, my role has centered on organizational design, headcount planning, budgeting, performance management, coaching managers and Staff+ engineers, technical and product strategy, executive stakeholder management, and establishing durable ownership for complex platform work.

- Lead a 30+ engineer identity and security platform organization through three direct Engineering Managers, with two of those leaders also managing additional Engineering Managers. Scope spans Provisioning Product + Platform, Identity Platform, [Machine Identity / AI Identity](https://1password.com/solutions/ai-agent-identity), and Confidential Computing.
- Built 1Password's [Confidential Computing](https://1password.com/blog/confidential-computing) platform and engineering team from the ground up, starting with a five-person R&D group of Staff+ engineers and reaching a production-grade platform in about a year. Led the IC team through development, hired its Engineering Manager, and supported the organization through GA, its first production use cases, and subsequent key-persistence capabilities; represented the work externally at [AWS re:Invent](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QBQawVrxqXg).
- Expanded leadership across Authentication, Provisioning, Identity, and Authorization, repeatedly establishing clearer ownership, durable leadership structures, and long-term operating models as the mandate grew.
- Reset Authentication around resiliency and operational stability through staffing, organizational, and technical-priority changes, materially improving reliability and significantly reducing production incidents; hired the team's Engineering Manager to establish sustainable leadership.
- Led engineering for [Automated Provisioning](https://support.1password.com/provisioning/) across technical direction, roadmap, execution, GA readiness, and operational reliability; reorganized team ownership and operating practices to improve delivery predictability. The product reached GA in March 2026.
- Built a new Identity organization in 2025, initially leading seven engineers directly before hiring its Engineering Manager; later expanded the mandate to include Authorization and led development of the [Agentic Identity and Authorization strategy and vision](https://1password.com/blog/introducing-1password-unified-access).
- Contributed at company level through Staff+ leadership development, hiring and interview-system improvements, engineering career frameworks, and technical culture.

### Engineering Leader, Connectivity @ Panasonic Avionics {#panasonic-avionics}

⌛ 2018–2021
{: .dates}

Panasonic Avionics' Connectivity business delivers global in-flight internet through multi-orbit satellite and terrestrial networks, aircraft connectivity systems, and cloud-based cabin services for airlines worldwide.
{: .company}

- Led within a 30+ engineer Connectivity organization through three managers across North America and Europe, delivering in-flight connectivity and e-commerce software for 300+ airline customers.
- Helped transition engineering from reactive bespoke customer work toward a more scalable platform/SaaS operating model, improving delivery velocity, quality, and customer confidence in the roadmap.
- Led automation of business-critical data collection and reporting, producing meaningful revenue gains and operational-efficiency improvements.
- Identified emerging PSD2/payment-compliance risk, authored and presented the response to the Panasonic Avionics C-suite, and drove formation of the cross-functional hardware/software effort used to mitigate revenue risk.

### Senior Manager, Web Development @ NCSOFT {#ncsoft}

⌛ 2011–2018
{: .dates}

NCSOFT is the world's premier publisher and developer of online games across multiple platforms (PC, Mobile, Console, and VR).
